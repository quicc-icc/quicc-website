Two former QuICC members, **Gabriel Senno** and **Ignacio Perito**, returned to the Faculty of Exact and Natural Sciences (FCEyN, UBA) in August and September 2026 to teach the course **“Generación cuántica de aleatoriedad: desde dispositivos actuales a esquemas basados en no-localidad”** (*Quantum randomness generation: from current devices to nonlocality-based schemes*). The course was part of the [Visiting Professors Program](https://www.dc.uba.ar/profesores-visitantes/) of the Department of Computer Science, which since 2011 has invited researchers and professionals to teach short courses for undergraduate and graduate students and to build collaborations with the department.

The outcomes of measurements on a quantum system can be completely unpredictable even when its state is perfectly known. This intrinsic randomness is the basis of the emerging industry of quantum random number generators. The course introduced the quantum formalism and the theory of certifying quantum states and measurements, with a focus on certifying randomness, and gave students theoretical and computational tools to define and quantify it under different levels of trust in the devices involved.

Topics included:

- Nonlocality and quantum certification
- Device-dependent, device-independent, and semi-device-independent quantification of randomness, including the Navascués–Pironio–Acín hierarchy
- From one to many uses of a quantum entropy source: individual, collective, and general attacks
- Randomness extraction

Both lecturers did their PhDs at FCEyN as members of QuICC, Gabriel in Computer Science (2017) and Ignacio in Physics (2020), and later worked as postdoctoral researchers in the quantum information group at the Institute of Photonic Sciences (ICFO) in Barcelona. Gabriel is now a research scientist in the R&D department of Quside, a company that develops quantum random number generators. Ignacio is a professor and researcher at La Salle University in Barcelona, where he works on nonlocality and the certification of quantum systems.
