Two members of QuICC, **Gustavo M. Bosyk** and **Guido Bellomo**, are serving as Guest Editors of the Special Issue **“Quantum Resource Theories: Mathematical Structures and Quantum Applications”**, part of the *Quantum Information* section of [*Entropy*](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/entropy) (MDPI).

Quantum resource theories provide a common framework for asking what can be achieved when only certain states, operations, or measurements are freely available. The Special Issue aims to bring together contributions across the whole spectrum, from structural and mathematical results to concrete applications.

Topics of interest include, among others:

- Axiomatic foundations and general structure of resource theories
- State convertibility and partial orders, such as majorization and thermomajorization
- Resource monotones, entropic quantities, and information-theoretic measures
- Convex geometry and optimization methods
- Specific resources: entanglement, coherence, purity, asymmetry, athermality, magic, and contextuality
- Quantum thermodynamics and nonequilibrium resources
- Approximate, probabilistic, and catalytic transformations
- Resource interconversion and multi-resource theories
- Resource theories of channels, measurements, and processes
- Applications to communication, metrology, algorithms, and computation, including numerical and experimental work

**Submission deadline: 1 September 2027.** Authors may also send a tentative title and a short abstract (about 250 words) in advance for a preliminary assessment.

Full details, submission instructions, and the official flyer are available on the [Special Issue website](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/entropy/special_issues/XH0JC89WCV).
