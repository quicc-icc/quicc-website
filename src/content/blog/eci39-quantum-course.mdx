At the 39th edition of the Escuela de Ciencias Informáticas (ECI 39), held from **July 27 to July 31** and organized by the Department of Computer Science of the University of Buenos Aires, QuICC member **Gustavo M. Bosyk** (ICC, UBA-CONICET) taught the course **“Mayorización y sus aplicaciones en información cuántica”** (*Majorization and its applications in quantum information*).

The course presented majorization theory as a core mathematical tool of quantum information: how it characterizes entanglement, which state transformations are possible with local operations and classical communication (LOCC), how quantum resources are compared, and how uncertainty relations are formulated. It brought together linear algebra, matrix theory, information theory, and quantum mechanics.

The program was organized in three parts:

- **Foundations of majorization theory:** majorization as a partial order on probability vectors, Lorenz curves, doubly stochastic matrices, Schur-concave functions, generalized entropies, and the Schur–Horn theorem.
- **Mathematical foundations of quantum mechanics:** Dirac notation, the postulates of quantum theory, pure and mixed states, projective and generalized measurements, quantum maps, composite systems, partial trace, and the Schmidt decomposition.
- **Applications to quantum information and computing:** the Schrödinger mixing theorem, quantum entropies, majorization-based separability criteria, LOCC transformations and Nielsen's theorem, quantum teleportation, entanglement measures, and uncertainty relations.

The course was taught in Spanish and aimed at students and researchers in quantum information, quantum computing, and their mathematical foundations, with basic linear algebra and quantum mechanics as recommended background.

More information is available on the [course page](https://eci.dc.uba.ar/cursos-eci/n1-bosyk/) of the ECI.
